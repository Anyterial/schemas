    • entrytypes

      • Anyterial altermagnet screening result entry type (entrytype) - https://schemas.anyterial.se/defs/v0.1/entrytypes/altermagnet_screening_results

        An altermagnet screening result is the Anyterial altermagnets database's provider-specific main entity: one screened candidate material together with the high-throughput screening science determined for it (classification, spin-splitting metrics, electronic type, crustal abundance, magnetic phase and wave-class assignments, and the linked MAGNDATA symmetry variants), its generated band-structure/crystal-structure/Brillouin-zone figures, and the total energy of the workflow run that produced it. The screened crystal structure itself is a separate standard OPTIMADE structures entry, linked from this entry through a structures relationship; the reference DOIs are linked through a references relationship. The science, figure, and energy properties served on this entry type are declared through property definitions and are not enumerated here.

    • properties

      • altermagnets

        • Average spin splitting (property) - https://schemas.anyterial.se/defs/v0.1/properties/altermagnets/avg_spin_splitting

          The Brillouin-zone average of the per-k-point largest spin splitting, evaluated within an energy window of plus or minus 3 eV around the Fermi level.

        • Screening source classification (property) - https://schemas.anyterial.se/defs/v0.1/properties/altermagnets/classification

          The source classification assigned to the material by the altermagnet material store from the kinds of its linked symmetry-variant sources.

        • Electronic type (property) - https://schemas.anyterial.se/defs/v0.1/properties/altermagnets/electronic_type

          A qualitative classification of the electronic structure of the material.

        • Screening elements (property) - https://schemas.anyterial.se/defs/v0.1/properties/altermagnets/elements_present

          The alphabetized chemical element symbols extracted from the formula recorded by the altermagnet screening workflow.

        • Material figures (property) - https://schemas.anyterial.se/defs/v0.1/properties/altermagnets/figures

          Data-only figure metadata for the fixed band-structure, crystal-structure, and Brillouin-zone figures of the material.

        • Screening formula (property) - https://schemas.anyterial.se/defs/v0.1/properties/altermagnets/formula

          The chemical formula recorded by the altermagnet screening workflow, served verbatim as a display string.

        • ICSD identifiers (property) - https://schemas.anyterial.se/defs/v0.1/properties/altermagnets/icsd_ids

          The ordered, deduplicated Inorganic Crystal Structure Database identifiers collected from the material's linked symmetry variants.

        • MAGNDATA symmetry variants (property) - https://schemas.anyterial.se/defs/v0.1/properties/altermagnets/magndata_variants

          The ordered list of symmetry variants resolved for the material from its linked MAGNDATA records, one item per record and symmetry-detection tolerance.

        • Magnetic phase (property) - https://schemas.anyterial.se/defs/v0.1/properties/altermagnets/magnetic_phase

          The magnetic phase assigned to the material by collinear/noncollinear symmetry analysis.

        • Magnetic phases (property) - https://schemas.anyterial.se/defs/v0.1/properties/altermagnets/magnetic_phases

          The full ordered, deduplicated magnetic-phase list collected from the material's linked symmetry variants.

        • Maximum spin splitting (property) - https://schemas.anyterial.se/defs/v0.1/properties/altermagnets/max_spin_splitting

          The largest spin splitting of the electronic bands found within an energy window of plus or minus 3 eV around the Fermi level.

        • Minimum crustal abundance (property) - https://schemas.anyterial.se/defs/v0.1/properties/altermagnets/min_crustal_abundance

          The minimum crustal abundance among the constituent elements of the material, given in parts per million by mass.

        • Parent space groups (property) - https://schemas.anyterial.se/defs/v0.1/properties/altermagnets/parent_spacegroups

          The full ordered, deduplicated parent-space-group list collected from the material's linked symmetry variants.

        • Screening rank (property) - https://schemas.anyterial.se/defs/v0.1/properties/altermagnets/screening_rank

          The material's ordinal position in the published high-throughput screening table, starting at 1.

        • Normalized search text (property) - https://schemas.anyterial.se/defs/v0.1/properties/altermagnets/search_text

          The normalized lowercase free-text aggregate maintained by the altermagnet material store, concatenating the formula, MAGNDATA identifiers, space groups, elements, magnetic phases, wave classes, and the source classification.

        • Screening space group (property) - https://schemas.anyterial.se/defs/v0.1/properties/altermagnets/space_group

          The space-group display string recorded by the altermagnet screening workflow, served verbatim.

        • Normalized screening space group (property) - https://schemas.anyterial.se/defs/v0.1/properties/altermagnets/space_group_search

          The screening space-group display string normalized by stripping surrounding whitespace and lowercasing.

        • Spin splitting fraction (property) - https://schemas.anyterial.se/defs/v0.1/properties/altermagnets/spin_splitting_fraction

          A dimensionless relative spin-splitting measure in the range 0 to 1.

        • Wave class (property) - https://schemas.anyterial.se/defs/v0.1/properties/altermagnets/wave_class

          The simplified spin-momentum wave class of the altermagnetic order.

        • Wave classes (property) - https://schemas.anyterial.se/defs/v0.1/properties/altermagnets/wave_classes

          The full ordered, deduplicated spin-momentum wave-class list collected from the material's linked symmetry variants.
